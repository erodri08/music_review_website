Featured Song

Today's featured song is Pearls by Sade. This song is one of my all time favorites! It was released on Sade's 1992 album "Love Deluxe". It touches upon themes of inequality, putting the strength and quiet dignity of human resilience on full display against the backdrop of suffering.
