My Top 10 Albums of All Time

2025
1

Blonde

Frank Ocean

Three years of silence, then a surprise release that redefined what a pop album could be. With themes of unrequited love, coming of age, and personal growth, Blonde feels like a collection of stories from the life of the often elusive artist. Favorites: Self Control, White Ferrari, Seigfreid.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
2

Vespertine

Björk

Whispered vocals, micro-beats, Icelandic choirs — the wide emotional spectrum of new love, oddly warm and cold at once. Favorites: Hidden Place, It's Not Up to You, Aurora.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
3

Lemonade

Beyoncé

A visual album that let the world in — grief, anger, forgiveness and resurrection across seventeen unflinching tracks. Best experienced in one sitting. Favorites: Don't Hurt Yourself, Sorry, Sandcastles.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
4

Ray of Light

Madonna

Trip-hop, techno and ambient soundscapes exploring motherhood and spiritual awakening — still sounds futuristic almost thirty years on. Favorites: Substitute for Love, Nothing Really Matters, Shanti Ashtangi.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
5

The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill

Ms. Lauryn Hill

Groundbreaking hip-hop, soul, R&B and reggae — a legacy album sampled by Drake, Cardi B and dozens more. Favorites: Lost Ones, Doo Wop, Nothing Even Matters.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
6

Mr. Morale & The Big Steppers

Kendrick Lamar

Not the triumphant return fans expected — an hour-long therapy session that strips away the saviour persona. Favorites: United in Grief, Rich Spirit, Mother I Sober.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
7

Dummy

Portishead

A landmark of trip-hop — strings, samples and Beth Gibbons' devastated vocals. Perfect for a rainy day. Favorites: Mysterons, Sour Times, Wandering Star.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
8

IGOR

Tyler, the Creator

Unrequited love cloaked in danceable, upbeat production — Tyler subverting expectations at every turn. Favorites: Running Out of Time, Puppet, Are We Still Friends?

Listen on Spotify Full Review
9

Late Registration

Kanye West

Lush strings, systemic critique, and the devastating "Hey Mama." My appreciation is purely musical. Favorites: Touch the Sky, Drive Slow, Diamonds From Sierra Leone.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
10

Revolver

The Beatles

Built for the studio, not the stage — pushing pop music into new territory with loneliness, alienation, and spiritual exploration. Favorites: Eleanor Rigby, Here There and Everywhere, For No One.

Listen on Spotify Full Review
